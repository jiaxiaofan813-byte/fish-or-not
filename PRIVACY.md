# Fish or Not Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy describes the current Fish or Not app for iPhone and iPad, maintained through the [Fish or Not project](https://github.com/jiaxiaofan813-byte/fish-or-not).

## Your information in the app

Fish or Not does not require an account. Your fishing preferences, favorite waters, gear entries, and manually selected starting point are saved locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives a copy of this profile. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

If you choose Use current location, the app requests location access while in use. It uses a location fix to find a starting point and calculate driving estimates. The app does not request background location access or save GPS starting points in the persistent profile. The current GPS starting point may remain in memory during the app session. You may instead search for a city or address without granting GPS access.

## Maps, forecasts, and network requests

- **Apple Maps / MapKit:** Location searches, map display, driving estimates, and navigation use Apple services. Depending on the feature, Apple processes search text, starting-point coordinates, and destination coordinates. Opening navigation hands the destination and, for a manually selected origin, that origin to Apple Maps. See [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/).
- **NOAA / National Weather Service:** Forecast requests use coordinates of public waters or access locations, including the reference water used for county-level weather comparisons. The app does not send your starting-point coordinates, gear, or saved profile to NWS. Network providers can receive connection information such as your IP address and request details. See the [National Weather Service privacy policy](https://www.weather.gov/privacy).
- **Reference links:** If you open a DNR source, Wikipedia, a photograph source, or another external link, that website receives the request and handles information under its own privacy policy. Fish photographs are bundled in the app; browsing them does not require downloading images from their source websites.

The developer has not added advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs to this version. The app does not sell your personal information or send your profile to an AI service. If you opt to share a fishing plan, the information in that plan is passed to the app or recipient you choose through the system share sheet.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Preferences, favorite waters, gear, and a manually selected starting point remain on the device until you edit them or delete the app. Driving estimates are held in memory and reused for up to five minutes. Public weather responses are stored in the app's cache; forecast freshness limits control whether they can be reused, and cached files may remain until the system clears them or the app is deleted. No personal-profile retention period is maintained on a developer server because this version has no such server.

You can change location permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Fish or Not. You can edit gear and preferences or remove favorites inside the app. To remove all locally stored app data, use Delete App in device settings, rather than Offload App. Backups and information processed by Apple, NWS, or a sharing destination are managed separately under those services' settings and policies.

## Support and privacy questions

Contact the project maintainer through the [Fish or Not support issue tracker](https://github.com/jiaxiaofan813-byte/fish-or-not/issues). GitHub processes your account and submitted content under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Issues are public: do not include your exact location, home address, or other sensitive information. You can ask how to arrange a private follow-up without posting the sensitive details.

## Policy updates

Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. This policy describes the current app's practices; external service providers maintain their own policies.
